# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Koi-Code-Aquarium to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@azizulabedinazmi](https://github.com/azizulabedinazmi), through a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or a direct message. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
